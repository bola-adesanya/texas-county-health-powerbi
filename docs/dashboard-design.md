# Dashboard design

## Page 1 — Data Quality Overview

Purpose: Show that the dataset is loaded, complete, and usable.

Recommended visuals:

- Card: total rows
- Card: distinct counties
- Card: distinct years
- Card: missing county names
- Card: missing rate values
- Table: row count by year

## Page 2 — County Population Explorer

Purpose: Allow users to inspect county population by year.

Recommended visuals:

- Year slicer
- County slicer
- Population card
- County population table
- Bar chart: top counties by population

## Page 3 — Demographic Extremes

Purpose: Identify counties with the highest percentage by demographic category.

Recommended visuals:

- Table: highest Anglo, Black, Hispanic, Other, female, and male percentages
- Bar chart: selected demographic percentage by county

## Page 4 — Mortality & Injury Extremes

Purpose: Show counties with highest and lowest public health outcome rates.

Recommended visuals:

- Table: heart disease high/low
- Table: lung cancer high/low
- Table: top/bottom 5 motor vehicle injury rates
- Table: top/bottom 5 suicide rates

## Page 5 — Public Assistance & Birth Outcomes

Purpose: Connect public assistance, poverty, insurance, and birth outcomes.

Recommended visuals:

- Bar chart: food stamp utilization by county
- Table: highest utilization county
- Column chart: low birth weight micro-average by uninsured-rate band

## Page 6 — Infectious Disease Trends

Purpose: Analyze county-level infectious disease activity over time.

Recommended visuals:

- Line chart: disease cases by year
- Matrix: county by disease
- Table: biggest single-year pertussis jump

## Page 7 — Export Tables

Purpose: Prepare structured output tables for XML and JSON use cases.

Recommended visuals:

- Employment extract table
- Infectious disease long-format table
