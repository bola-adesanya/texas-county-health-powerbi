# LinkedIn post draft

I converted a biomedical informatics public health data-access assignment into a Power BI portfolio project: **Texas County Health Analytics Dashboard**.

The dashboard analyzes Texas county-level public health data across population, demographics, mortality, injury, infectious disease, food assistance, poverty, insurance coverage, low birth weight, and employment indicators.

What I focused on:

- Building a reusable Power BI data model from yearly county CSV files
- Cleaning and validating public health data in Power Query
- Creating DAX measures for utilization rates, demographic rankings, and weighted health outcomes
- Calculating low birth weight using a micro-average instead of a simple county average
- Identifying counties with extreme mortality, injury, and disease-rate patterns
- Translating academic data-access tasks into an analytics product a stakeholder could use

This was a useful exercise in turning raw public health data into decision-ready insight, with emphasis on data quality, reproducibility, and stakeholder storytelling.
